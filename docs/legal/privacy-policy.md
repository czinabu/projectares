Verge is made by [Caleb's legal name or studio name] ("we"). This policy
says what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** When you play online, our servers keep your player name,
  a secret your game makes to sign you in (we keep only a scrambled copy of
  any recovery code), and your progress: your stash, loadout, base, scrip,
  mastery, clan, friends and squad. [If Steam login ships: your Steam ID.]
- **Connection data.** Our servers see your IP address while you are
  connected, to send you the game. [Say whether server logs keep it, and for
  how long.]
- **What you say and do in the game.** Chat, clan names and what happens in
  a match, as the game needs to run it.
- **Bug reports, only if you send one.** The phone's Report app sends what
  you type, where you were in the game, and the game's log files to our
  private Discord channel. Logs can include your player name.
- **On your computer only.** Your solo save, settings and copies of the bug
  reports you sent stay on your computer and are never sent to us.

We do not show ads, sell your data, or share it with anyone for marketing.

## Who else handles it

- **Our server host** [name, country], which runs the game servers.
- **Discord**, which holds the bug reports you choose to send.
- **Valve (Steam)**, under Steam's own privacy policy, when you buy or
  launch the game through Steam.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is in use. We keep rolling backups of the
account database for [N] days. If you ask us to delete your account, we
delete it and it falls out of the backups within [N] days.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your account data by writing to
[contact email]. Tell us your player name and, if you can, sign in so we
know the account is yours. If you live in the EU, UK or California, you
have these rights by law, and you can also complain to your local data
protection authority.

## Children

Verge is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us
and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and say so in the
game's update notes.
