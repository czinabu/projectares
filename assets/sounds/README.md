# Recorded sounds

Clips cut from the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC 2026 bundle (royalty-free, commercial
use, no attribution). Its license lets them ship inside the game but not be
handed out as they are, so the `.ogg` files are not in this public repository.
Put them here by hand; without them `Sfx` plays its synthesized voices.

| File | Plays as | Cut from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wind_storm.ogg` | `wind` (38 s loop) | 344 Audio, Extreme Winds Vol. 1: "Eye Of The Storm", 20 to 58 s, crossfaded into a loop |
| `war_rumble.ogg` | `war_rumble` (24 s loop) | Cinematic Sound Design, Sci-Fi Drones: "Dark Industrial Ambience", 4 to 28 s, low-passed at 500 Hz, looped |
| `far_boom_1.ogg` to `far_boom_3.ogg` | `far_boom` (taken at random) | Federico Soler, Effective Trailer Booms Vol. 2: 011, 075, 214, first 7 s, faded, low-passed at 3.5 kHz, mono |
